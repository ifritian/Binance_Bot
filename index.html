Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. These are market observations and records of an automated method. Crypto prices swing hard and you can lose money. Decide for yourself.

Who writes the posts?

An automated system builds them from Binance market data and public announcements. Numbers come from code, not from a person typing them. The owner reads and answers comments.

Are the trend ideas profitable?

Unproven. In historical tests the method's edge was small and statistically weak, so this page exists to measure it in real time instead of promising anything. The table above is the answer, whatever it turns out to be.

What about fees?

Results are shown after an estimated 0.2% fee per round trip. Real fills can differ.

Is anything paid or promoted?

No coin promotions and no paid placements. The project can earn in two ways: Binance Square pays a commission when readers click a coin tag and trade, and the "Open a Binance account" button and some post links are referral links, which pay a share of trading fees if you sign up through them. It costs you nothing extra.
